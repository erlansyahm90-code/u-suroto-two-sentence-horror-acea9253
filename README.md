## The story

> At noon, my phone buzzed with a voicemail from my own number, timestamped 3 AM tonight. It was my voice calmly listing everything I would do this evening, and it ended with the sound of my front door opening.

Exactly two sentences, 207 characters. A voicemail from the narrator's own
number, timestamped in the future — their own voice calmly listing everything
they will do this evening, ending with the sound of their front door opening.
The second sentence turns the first: what first reads as a prank becomes a
prediction that ends in intrusion. The horror is implied, never explained —
no gore, no familiar twist.
